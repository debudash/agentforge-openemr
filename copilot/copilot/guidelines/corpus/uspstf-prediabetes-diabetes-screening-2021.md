## Recommendation

Population: Asymptomatic adults aged 35 to 70 years who have overweight or obesity.

The USPSTF recommends screening for prediabetes and type 2 diabetes in adults aged 35 to 70 years who have overweight or obesity.

Grade: B.

## Screening tests

Prediabetes and type 2 diabetes can be detected by measuring fasting plasma glucose or HbA1c level, or with an oral glucose tolerance test.

## Diagnostic thresholds

Type 2 diabetes: fasting plasma glucose 126 mg/dL or greater, HbA1c 6.5% or greater, or 2-hour postload glucose 200 mg/dL or greater.

Prediabetes: fasting plasma glucose 100 to 125 mg/dL, HbA1c 5.7% to 6.4%, or 2-hour postload glucose 140 to 199 mg/dL.

## Screening interval

Screening every 3 years may be a reasonable approach for adults with normal blood glucose levels.
